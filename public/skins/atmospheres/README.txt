Starfall is still rendered in code, not as a video.

Recommended asset spec:
- 1920x1080 or 2560x1440
- 8 to 12 second seamless loop
- muted, no audio track
- webm preferred, mp4 fallback
- avoid hard cuts
- keep motion elegant and slow
